Drop Testing & Analysis

Summary of the electronics for the Robot:

The program runs on a loop where the board is constantly checking the position of the board, indicating proper orientation for launch. If the robot is on its side, the board will indicate an orientation error via a LED located on the top of the robot. However, if the orientation is correct the motor will begin winding the fishing line until it approaches the release mechanism. At which point, the board will check its position one last time and do the final winding to initiate the release, sending the robot flying.

Objectives

Performance

Jumps 3ft to 10ft on Earth, simulating up to 60ft lunar leaps on the moon.

Intelligence

Autonomous operation, wireless image transmission, and self-resetting. All allow for the operator to set the robot down, step away and capture a wide-view real time video of the lunar surface.

Durability

Designed for repeated lunar impacts, extreme temperature variations, and operations in harsh lunar dust.
